The seven layouts

Above the flip dealers are long gamma (they sell strength, buy weakness: quieter, mean-reverting); below it they are short gamma (they chase price: faster, trending). Charts: green = CALL WALL · purple = PUT WALL · orange dashed = GAMMA FLIP · line = price (dashed = the other way it can go) · POS / NEG = gamma side of the flip. Regime: net / gross GEX above +2% = positive, below −2% = negative, in between = neutral.
